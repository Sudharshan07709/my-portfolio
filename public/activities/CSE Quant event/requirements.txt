I participated in the Quant & Code Hackathon organized by the Informatics Department at TUM as part of the Computational Science and Engineering program. The event gave me hands-on exposure to problem-solving in areas such as quantitative analysis, coding under time constraints, and working with unfamiliar problem statements.

Although my team did not win, the hackathon was a valuable learning experience.
