As a student at the Goethe-Institut Chennai, I led a team of five students to organize the Goethe–Kafka Event, focused on the works of Franz Kafka, especially Die Verwandlung (The Metamorphosis). We designed interactive sessions for the public and school students, including VR experiences, creative craft activities, and game-based quests to make Kafka’s ideas more engaging and accessible.

I was involved in planning, coordination, and execution of the event, working closely with the Goethe-Institut library team. This experience strengthened my leadership, teamwork, and communication skills while allowing me to combine literature, creativity, and technology in an educational setting.
